# Reconnection

## Use When

Use this when you have not talked to someone in a long time and want to reopen the relationship without pretending the gap did not happen.

## Subject Lines

- Long time — hello
- Reconnecting
- Thought I’d say hello
- It’s been a while

## Email Template

Hi {{name}},

It has been a while, and I wanted to reach out and say hello.

I was thinking about {{specific_memory_or_context}} and wondered how you have been.

A lot has changed on my side since we last talked, but I’ll keep this simple: I hope you are doing well.

No pressure to reply quickly. I just wanted to reconnect.

Chris

## Short Text / WhatsApp Version

Hi {{name}}, it has been a while and I wanted to say hello. I was thinking about {{specific_memory_or_context}} and wondered how you have been.

## Business Reconnection Version

Hi {{name}},

It has been a while, and I wanted to reconnect.

I was thinking about our earlier conversation around {{specific_topic}} and wondered how things developed on your side.

No agenda — just wanted to say hello and see how things are going.

Chris

## Personalization Notes

Name the gap lightly. Do not over-apologize unless you truly owe them an apology.

Use a real memory or context. Without that, reconnection messages feel like list-cleaning.
