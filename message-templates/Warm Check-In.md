# Warm Check-In

## Use When

Use this when you want to stay in touch without pitching, asking, or making the person feel like they are on a list.

## Subject Lines

- Quick hello
- Thought of you
- Checking in
- Hope you’re doing well

## Email Template

Hi {{name}},

I was thinking of you and wanted to say hello.

How have things been with {{specific_topic}}?

No rush to reply. I just wanted to check in and hope things are going well on your side.

Chris

## Short Text / WhatsApp Version

Hi {{name}}, I was thinking of you and wanted to say hello. How have things been with {{specific_topic}}?

## Personalization Notes

Replace `{{specific_topic}}` with something real from their contact note, such as a project, trip, family update, health update, launch, or conversation you had.

Do not send this unchanged. A generic check-in feels lazy.
