# Warm Check-In

## Use When

Use this when you want to stay present in someone’s life without asking for anything.

The purpose is simple: show genuine care, remember what matters to them, and keep the relationship warm through attention.

## Subject Lines

- Thought of you
- Quick hello
- Checking in on {{specific_topic}}
- Hope things are moving well

## Email Template

Hi {{name}},

I was thinking about our last conversation around {{specific_topic}} and wanted to check in.

How has that been unfolding for you?

No agenda on my side. I just value staying connected and wanted to see how things are going.

Chris

## Short Text / WhatsApp Version

Hi {{name}}, I was thinking about our last conversation around {{specific_topic}} and wanted to check in. How has that been unfolding for you?

## More Generous Version

Hi {{name}},

I was thinking about {{specific_topic}} and wondered how things are going.

If there is anything useful I can send your way — a resource, a contact, or even a second set of eyes — I’m happy to help if I can.

Chris

## Personalization Notes

Replace `{{specific_topic}}` with something real from their contact note.

A good check-in proves you remembered. A weak check-in sounds like a mass email.
