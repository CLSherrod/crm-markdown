# Make an Introduction

## Use When

Use this when you want to introduce two people who may genuinely benefit from knowing each other.

A good introduction is a gift of trust. Make the reason clear and then step back.

## Subject Lines

- Introducing {{person_a}} and {{person_b}}
- Thought you two should meet
- Useful connection
- Introduction: {{person_a}} <> {{person_b}}

## Email Template

Hi {{person_a}} and {{person_b}},

I wanted to introduce you both because I think there may be a useful connection around {{shared_reason}}.

{{person_a}}, meet {{person_b}}. {{one_sentence_about_person_b}}

{{person_b}}, meet {{person_a}}. {{one_sentence_about_person_a}}

I’ll let you both take it from here.

Chris

## Short Text / WhatsApp Version

Hi {{person_a}} and {{person_b}}, I wanted to introduce you because I think there may be a useful connection around {{shared_reason}}. I’ll let you both take it from here.

## Permission-First Version

Hi {{name}},

I know someone I think may be useful for you to meet around {{topic}}.

Would you be open to an introduction?

No worries if your plate is full.

Chris

## Personalization Notes

Use permission-first introductions when the relationship is sensitive, high-value, or busy.

Do not introduce people just to look helpful. Make sure there is a real reason.
