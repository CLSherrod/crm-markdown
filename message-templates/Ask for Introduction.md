# Ask for Introduction

## Use When

Use this when you want someone to introduce you to another person.

Make the request easy to understand, easy to decline, and easy to forward.

## Subject Lines

- Quick introduction question
- Possible introduction?
- Would you be comfortable introducing me?
- Small ask

## Email Template

Hi {{name}},

I wanted to ask a small favor.

Would you be comfortable introducing me to {{person_name}}?

I think there may be a useful overlap around {{reason_for_introduction}}, and I believe I may be able to offer something helpful around {{value_you_can_offer}}.

If it does not feel like a fit, no worries at all.

If you are open to it, I can send you a short forwardable note so it is easy on your side.

Chris

## Short Text / WhatsApp Version

Hi {{name}}, would you be comfortable introducing me to {{person_name}}? I think there may be a useful overlap around {{reason_for_introduction}}. No worries if not.

## Forwardable Blurb

Hi {{person_name}},

I wanted to introduce you to Chris.

Chris does work around {{your_relevant_work}}, and I thought there might be a useful connection around {{shared_context}}.

I’ll let you two take it from here.

## Personalization Notes

Do not make the other person do the work. Offer a forwardable note.

A good introduction request protects the relationship first.
