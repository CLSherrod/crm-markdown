# Relationship Workflow Reference

## Follow-up

Log the interaction, update `last_contacted`, write a specific `contact_reason`, and choose `next_contact_due` deliberately. Do not repeatedly move a reminder date without recording why.

## Reviews

Weekly reviews are lightweight resets for replies owed and one thoughtful action. Monthly reviews reassess relationship priorities, tiers, neglected relationships, introductions, gifts, and archival decisions.

## Companies

Keep shared organization context in company notes. Keep personal details and communication history in individual contact notes. Link both directions when useful.

## Privacy

Minimize personal data, redact source material before AI processing when possible, and never include private records in samples or public examples.
