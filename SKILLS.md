# CRM AI Skills

This repository contains a reusable AI skill for working with the CRM in a careful, human-first way.

The canonical skill instructions are in [`skills/crm-markdown/SKILL.md`](skills/crm-markdown/SKILL.md).

Use the skill for requests such as:

- Create or update a contact from notes, email, transcripts, or business cards.
- Extract to-dos and follow-up reminders.
- Draft a message or request missing contact information.
- Review an AI-generated or imported contact.
- Find duplicate, incomplete, overdue, or inactive contacts.
- Prepare weekly or monthly relationship reviews.

The skill requires human review before AI-generated or imported records become active contacts.
