# Networking Dashboard

Use this page as your daily relationship check-in. The reusable views live in
`Follow-Up Dashboard.base`; use this page for the daily routine and monthly review.

The system is simple:

1. Contact the people due today.
2. Clear anything overdue.
3. Look ahead at this week.
4. Log the interaction inside that person’s note.
5. Update `last_contacted` and `next_contact_due`.

## Contact Today

Open `Follow-Up Dashboard.base` and use its **Contact Today** view.

## Overdue

Use the **Overdue** view in `Follow-Up Dashboard.base`.

## This Week

Use the **This Week** view in `Follow-Up Dashboard.base`.

## Monthly Review

Once a month, duplicate `templates/Monthly Review Template.md` and answer the prompts.

The goal is not to contact everyone. The goal is to maintain the relationships that still matter.
